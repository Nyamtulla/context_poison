Experiment 3  ·  COIECD vs corpus poisoning

COIECD changes nothing we can measure

What its paper claims

An information-entropy constraint detects when the retrieved passage conflicts with the model’s prior, and shifts the output toward the passage where it fires.

What we ran

Its released code, n = 400, same items and dosing as the others. This is also one of our two deliberate repeat-controls: its own published pairing was re-run to check the harness.

What we got

Eight comparisons, the largest 1.8 points against a 9.9 threshold, no p below 0.20. No resolvable effect in either direction.

n = 400, threshold 9.9pp. “No resolvable effect” is not the same as “no effect” — it means this sample size cannot see one.

Worth being precise about the wording. We never say a defense has no effect; we say the run cannot resolve one at that sample size. Several published claims in this area rest on differences a run of their size cannot resolve. The committed prediction was wrong here in an informative direction: we expected COIECD to lose ground like ParamMute because it is the same family. It does not, because its entropy constraint simply does not fire the way we assumed.
