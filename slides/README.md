# The advisor deck

The rendered deck lives at https://claude.ai/artifact/VePveKZUe6mYKbqUK9gyMf
and is private until shared from that page. These are its sources, kept here
so the deck is reviewable and recoverable alongside the paper rather than
living only in one web account.

`deck.json` is the index: slide order, section outline, typefaces. One
`<id>.html` per slide, each holding a single `<section>` plus an `<aside>` of
speaker notes. The notes carry the caveats, the committed predictions and the
answers to the questions each slide invites, so they are worth reading even
if you never present it.

**The cube figure is not in here.** Slide 3 references an image uploaded to
the artifact's asset store by its `/_blob/` id. The source is
`latex/figures/fig1_cube.pdf`, generated by `scripts/gen_paper_figures.py`.
To refresh it after the figure changes:

    pdftoppm -png -r 300 -singlefile latex/figures/fig1_cube.pdf cube

then upload `cube.png` to the artifact and point the `<img src>` at the id it
returns. The slide and Figure 4 are deliberately the same image, so they
cannot drift.

**Numbers in these slides are copied, not generated.** Unlike the paper,
which reads every count from `latex/numbers.tex`, a slide says `236` because
someone typed it. After any corpus or registry rebuild, re-check the cube
slide, the roster slides and the cover against `latex/numbers.tex`.
