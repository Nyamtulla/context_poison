Experiment 2  ·  SpARE vs corpus poisoning

SpARE repeats the pattern, and the sign flips at both ends

What its paper claims

Editing activations to steer the model toward the retrieved context raises accuracy when the context and the model’s memory disagree. Validated on knowledge conflict only.

What we ran

Its released code, n = 500. SpARE returns both steering directions for every item from one model and one prompt, so the comparison is internal.

What we got

Steering toward the context is worth +14.2 when the context is honest and costs −10.2 when it is attacked. Both ends clear the threshold.

accuracy on the gold answersteer→memorysteer→contextdifference
clean context39.653.8+14.2
10% poisoned28.833.0+4.2
50% poisoned22.615.4−7.2
100% poisoned12.01.8−10.2

n = 500, threshold 8.9pp. The clearest case: the measurement that shows the defense works is the one that later convicts it.

Two defenses, no shared code, same shape. That is what makes this a property of the design rather than of one paper. State the caveat yourself: this run carries no undefended arm, so it compares two settings of one dial rather than a defense against its absence. The paper marks the row. Why both ends clearing threshold matters: ParamMute gave a resolvable reading at one end only. This one gives it at both, so the flip is not an artefact of a weak measurement.
