Experiment 7  ·  RobustRAG vs PoisonedRAG, then corpus poisoning

RobustRAG stops its own attack and misses the next one

What its paper claims

Answer over each retrieved passage in isolation and aggregate, so a single corrupted passage is outvoted. Published with certifiable guarantees against retrieval-corruption attacks.

What we ran

Its released code, Natural Questions with a real retrieval pool, n = 500. First against PoisonedRAG, which its own paper tested, then against corpus poisoning — same taxonomy cell, never run together.

What we got

It reproduces its published result, then recovers nothing against the neighbouring attack. Adding one line to refill the evidence window recovers 40.6 points.

This is the cleanest single illustration of why the taxonomy picks the pairs. Same channel in, same consequence out, and the defense is structurally blind to the second attack. The reason is in its own code: corpus poisoning displaces every honest passage out of the top-k window, so isolate-then-aggregate has nothing honest left to aggregate. The fix knows nothing about the attack, needs no tuning, and recovers 40.6 of a 53-point hole. It is also our second deliberate repeat-control: the PoisonedRAG row is a published pairing re-run to show the harness reproduces published results.
