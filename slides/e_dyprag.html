Experiment 6  ·  DyPRAG vs corpus poisoning

DyPRAG looks immune because it stopped reading the document

What its paper claims

Convert the retrieved passage into a small set of weights, inject them, and generate with the passage removed from the prompt — so there is no passage left for an attacker to own.

What we ran

Its released translator and checkpoints, n = 400, plus a closed-book arm that answers with no document at all.

What we got

Perfectly flat under an attack that destroys the undefended arm. The closed-book arm is statistically indistinguishable from it.

n = 400. Only a no-retrieval arm separates immunity from inattention, and no paper in this family reports one.

This is the clearest argument for the third arm, and it changed a conclusion we had already written down. All three committed predictions were confirmed. But the stated REASON was wrong: we said the attack's token-space precision cannot survive compression into a small weight update. Nothing survives it, the gold answer included. So a confirmed prediction can still be wrong underneath, and only the control shows it.
