Experiment 5  ·  SCR vs corpus poisoning

SCR buys a little under attack and pays a lot on clean data

What its paper claims

Show the model its own closed-book answer, the document, and the answer the document supports, and ask it to judge which to believe. The only method we ran whose design anticipates a deceptive document.

What we ran

Its released prompting pipeline, n = 400, same items and dosing. A no-retrieval arm runs alongside so we can tell reading from abstaining.

What we got

It does help under attack, +2.8 at saturation. It pays 13.5 points on clean data to buy that — roughly five times the benefit.

n = 400. The cost is resolvable at every dose below saturation. A defense deployed permanently and attacked occasionally is a poor trade at that rate.

The committed prediction was that SCR would degrade LESS than plain RAG, because the poisoned passage does not read like credible evidence and SCR is asked to notice. Falsified at every dose but saturation. The companion prediction held: +41.2 over closed-book on clean context, so this is a real defense being measured, not abstention in disguise. General caution: defenses that add a reasoning step are evaluated on whether they catch the attack. What is rarely reported is what the reasoning step costs when there is nothing to catch.
