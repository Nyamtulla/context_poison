The experiments point at one mechanism

One mechanism, not a list

Context-reliance defenses relocate correctness rather than creating it. What they take from memory the attacker receives; what they take from the attacker, memory receives. Neither direction moves anything toward the real document.

Zero benefit where it should be easiest

At the 10% dose, with nine tenths of the honest document intact, not one of 16 (defense, payload) pairs shows a resolvable benefit.

The one that resisted, priced

ACD gives up 29% of its protection to a template search and 58% to a gradient. It degrades rather than collapses, and the ceiling follows from its own rule.

Three controls changed our own conclusions. A no-retrieval arm turned our best result into inattention. An abstention axis found a safety gain invisible in accuracy. A model control reversed an effect’s sign.

Close on the asymmetry that makes the negative results trustworthy: an attack not built for a defense that beats it anyway is a lower bound on attack strength. So non-adaptive evaluation is conservative for a claim that a defense fails, and invalid for a claim that one holds. Every failure we report is of the first kind; the single resistance result is of the second, and we tested it adaptively rather than asserting it. If there is time for one ask, it is the RobustRAG x distractor run. All 18 of our crossings currently go safety-regime defense against security-regime attack; that run fills the other direction.
