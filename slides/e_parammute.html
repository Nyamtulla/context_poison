Experiment 1  ·  ParamMute vs corpus poisoning

ParamMute helps on clean context and hurts under attack

What its paper claims

Suppressing the FFN neurons that carry parametric memory makes the model follow the retrieved passage instead of its own prior. Validated on knowledge conflict only.

What we ran

Its released code and weights, CoConflictQA over Natural Questions, n = 1409. Corpus poisoning replaces 10%, 50% and 100% of the context, with a clean arm at 0%.

What we got

It reproduces its published benefit, then costs accuracy as soon as an adversary writes the context. The mechanism never stops working — that is the problem.

The headline test of the thesis: a defense built for context that goes bad by accident, run against context made bad on purpose. The control establishes first — +9.3 on its own benchmark with the inhibition logged firing — so every number under attack is readable. Across the whole dose range it loses 13.5 points more accuracy than doing nothing at all. Suppress the model's memory, and whatever owns the context inherits the answer.
