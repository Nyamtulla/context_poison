\section{Background and scope} \label{sec:background}

\subsection{What we mean by context poisoning}

\emph{Context poisoning} is our term for three things happening together.
Content enters an agent's context window. The agent should not have trusted
it. The agent's behavior changes as a result. The definition says nothing
about who put the content there, and that silence is deliberate: it is what
lets both communities fit inside one term.

The term is wider than prompt injection, which is one channel and one intent.
It is wider than knowledge conflict, which is one consequence. It is narrower
than ``LLM safety'', because the damage must arrive through the context
window. Two things fall outside it. Training-time poisoning and weight attacks
do not travel through the context. Jailbreaks in which the user is the
attacker have no third-party content at all.

\subsection{Two places an answer can come from} \label{sec:twoplaces}

A model answers from one of two sources. The first is the \emph{context}: the
document, tool output or message placed in front of it. The second is what the
model learned in training and holds in its weights. The safety literature calls that \emph{parametric memory}. We use the shorter
\emph{memory} for it throughout. Every result table reports it as
\texttt{pm\_acc}. One of the defenses we evaluate, ParamMute~\cite{huang-2025-parammute}, takes its name
from it.

This distinction carries the whole of Section~\ref{sec:results}.

\paragraph{Context-reliance defenses.} A large family of published methods
intervenes on exactly this distinction. A method is a \emph{context-reliance
defense} when its intervention is a \textbf{weighting between the model's
context-conditioned prediction and its memory-conditioned prediction}. The
membership test is mechanical. The method changes \emph{how much} the model
leans on retrieved text. It never asks \emph{which} retrieved text deserves
trust.

These methods build the dial differently, and it is the same dial. ACD decodes
from the no-context distribution and pulls it toward the with-context one by a
weight computed from entropy. CK-PLUG~\cite{bi-2025-parameters} interpolates the two distributions under
a single parameter. ParamMute suppresses the feed-forward layers that carry
parametric memory, forcing the model onto the retrieved text. SpARE~\cite{zhao-2024-steering} and SHIFT~\cite{li-2026-shift}
steer along a context-faithfulness direction in activation space. COIECD~\cite{yuan-2024-discerning}
detects a conflict between the two sources and pushes toward the context. SCR~\cite{huang-2024-trust}
makes the same trade through prompting rather than decoding.

The definition is narrow because the definition is what carries the result.
The dial runs between \emph{believe the context} and \emph{believe memory}. No
setting on it separates a real document from a planted one, because both are
context. Section~\ref{sec:distractor} measures what turning it does and does
not achieve.

The distinction is real in one respect and empty in another. It is real
because an adaptive adversary re-optimizes against a filter and a stale
retrieval index does not. A method tuned to the average case has no reason to
hold at the worst case. It is empty because the model cannot tell the
difference. A poisoned passage and a stale passage arrive through the same
channel, occupy the same positions, and corrupt the same reasoning. The
provenance differs; the token stream does not.

Our taxonomy therefore uses \emph{mechanism} as the neutral term for both, and
records provenance in a separate \emph{intent} axis. Writing "attack" at the
top level would have made the safety half unregisterable, because an
attack presupposes an attacker.

\subsection{The agent context as a set of inflow channels}

It helps to picture the context window as a body of water fed by a fixed set
of inflow streams. Whatever enters through any stream mixes into the same body
and affects everything downstream, whether it was dumped in deliberately or
arrived as runoff. The question this paper answers first is simple to state: how
many streams are there, and for each one, what stands guard at its mouth?

The picture needs one correction. Something outside a pond pollutes it, but
some of these channels feed themselves. A poisoned agent's output becomes
another agent's input, and poisoned memory reactivates in a later session. The
phenomenon is worse than the picture: contamination can propagate to other
agents and can re-enter the same agent from storage.
